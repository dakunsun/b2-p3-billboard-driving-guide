How to use this guide

Use the stop number to match the sheet and map. A north-facing face is normally checked while traveling south. IDs 1–20 follow this route and match the B2 Pre-Selected-P3 sheet and the two B2 vendor layers. Read side means the side of your vehicle: left is the driver’s side; right is the passenger’s side. A passenger should handle photos and check-offs while moving.

Every navigation link, including stop 1, starts from your current location. Google Maps may show one intermediate dropped pin: it shapes the viewing approach and is not another billboard or a parking stop. Your location can still be edited in Google Maps. Route maps show the modeled road path. Google Maps may choose another path; check the preview against the approach direction and exit notes below.

Preliminary visibility and affluence ratings are copied from the sheet on a 0–2 scale. Affluence describes a surrounding-area income indicator, not individual viewers. Completion is saved on this device. Use Export checks to keep a copy.
